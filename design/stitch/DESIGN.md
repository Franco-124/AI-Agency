## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance, precision-engineered visual identity for AI automation consulting targeting growth-focused small and medium enterprises. It combines the rigorous architectural discipline of enterprise infrastructure tools with the ambient, kinetic energy of generative artificial intelligence.

The visual style merges dark-mode minimalism with targeted glassmorphism and radiant violet ambient illumination. Deep space black canvas foundations create infinite depth, while translucent panels, micro-gradient stroke boundaries, and controlled optical blooms signal speed, automation, and technological precision. Interfaces feel tactile yet weightless, delivering a peerless consulting and software experience that commands enterprise trust while remaining completely accessible to regional business leaders.

## Colors

The palette leverages an obsidian base (`#0A0A12`) calibrated to absorb visual clutter and amplify luminescence. Chromatic hierarchy centers on three coordinated hues:
- **Primary Violet (`#7C3AED`):** The operational core used for primary CTAs, active states, and focal points.
- **Secondary Indigo (`#6366F1`):** The structural counter-weight driving multi-stop gradients, technical markers, and metrics callouts.
- **Tertiary Electric Lilac (`#A855F7`):** The glow and highlight layer, activating borders, focus rings, and badge treatments.

Contrast standards rigorously enforce WCAG AAA compliance for running text on all dark glass backgrounds. Gradients flow unidirectionally from Indigo (`#6366F1`) through Violet (`#7C3AED`) to Electric Lilac (`#A855F7`) at a 135-degree angle, reserved strictly for primary interactive drivers, hero titles, and ambient backdrop meshes.

## Typography

The typographic architecture balances technical authority with high-speed executive scannability. 

Headings rely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, utilizing tighter letter-tracking (-0.02em to -0.03em) and distinct geometric curves to assert leadership and modern agency craftsmanship. Key phrases within display headlines can apply a linear purple-to-lilac gradient fill via background-clip masks.

Body content and data tables run on **Inter**, delivering neutral, uncompromised legibility across density tiers. Numeric metrics and stat figures take heavy font weights with tabular numerals (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent layout shifts during interaction and dashboard visual transitions.

## Layout & Spacing

The system runs on a structural 12-column grid capped at a maximum width of 1280px, centered horizontally. Generous vertical rhythms (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) isolate sections, producing an uncluttered, high-end product posture.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, 24px gutters, dynamic margins bounded by the container limit.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 20px gutters, 32px screen edge margin. Complex comparative layouts collapse from 3-4 columns to dual columns.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 20px side padding. All multi-column cards stack linearly into single-column cards with persistent vertical spacing (`space-lg`).

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial dimension is established through back-blurs, luminescent rims, and controlled radial gradients rather than heavy opaque drop-shadows:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Solid `#0A0A12` with subtle, fixed SVG noise and ultra-diffuse radial violet glows (`rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.08)`, 600px blur radius) positioned behind major conversion modules.
- **Level 1 (Card & Container Surface):** Semi-opaque background (`rgba(18, 17, 36, 0.65)`) with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a precise 1px stroke running `rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.15)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Surfaces):** The background transitions to `rgba(26, 24, 52, 0.85)` while border opacity climbs to `rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.45)`, complemented by an external ambient bloom: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.25)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Sticky Navbars):** High-density glass (`rgba(10, 10, 18, 0.85)`) paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, delimited by a continuous 1px bottom edge `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework follows a cohesive 8px (`0.5rem`) base metric that harmonizes technical precision with approachable modern software aesthetics:

- **Inputs, Buttons, and Badges:** `rounded-md` (8px) to maintain crisp, tactile boundaries.
- **Cards, Pricing Modules, and Form Shells:** `rounded-lg` (16px) with an interior nested child component corner radius of 8px, maintaining geometric continuity.
- **Pill Badges & Indicator Tags:** Circular bounds (`rounded-full`) reserved strictly for micro-status indicators (e.g., active bots, platform connection tags, feature highlights).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Linear gradient fill (`135deg, #7C3AED, #6366F1`), white semibold typography, 1px top highlight border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`). On hover: 4px upward translation shadow bloom (`0 8px 24px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary (Ghost Glass):** Transparent background, 1px border (`rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.25)`), neutral light text. On hover: border shifts to `rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.6)` and background fills with `rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.1)`.

### Glass Cards & Stat Modules
- Built using 16px corner radii and backdrop blur.
- Include a 1px continuous hairline border using a linear gradient running from `rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.3)` at the top edge fading to `rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.05)` at the bottom edge, simulating overhead architectural lighting.
- Metric cards showcase `stat-display` numbers rendered in pure white or gradient mask, paired with a small uppercase label (`label-sm`) in muted slate.

### Lead Capture Booking Form
- **Form Panels:** Heavy elevation glass layer with 24px inner padding.
- **Input Fields:** Dark inset backgrounds (`#0E0D1B`), 1px neutral border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`), 12px vertical padding. Upon focus, the border shifts to tertiary lilac (`#A855F7`) accompanied by a subtle 3px glow ring (`rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.2)`).
- **Labels:** Crisp, high-contrast text positioned consistently above the input with explicit micro-copy requirements.

### Interactive FAQ Accordions
- Segmented list format with 1px divider lines (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
- Headers feature hover state background transitions to faint purple washes (`rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.05)`).
- Collapsible disclosure icons rotate 45 degrees smoothly via a 200ms cubic-bezier transition upon expansion.

### Plan & Pricing Comparison Matrix
- Highlighted plan cards utilize a persistent violet perimeter glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 40px -10px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.3)`) and an elevated badge tag fixed to the top border.
- Feature lists employ dedicated icon bullets: glowing green checkmarks (`#10B981`) for included capabilities and muted dashes for omitted elements.
